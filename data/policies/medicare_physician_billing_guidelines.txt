MEDICARE PHYSICIAN BILLING GUIDELINES AND COMPLIANCE STANDARDS

1. EVALUATION & MANAGEMENT (E/M) CODING
E/M codes (99202-99215) are the most frequently billed physician codes.

Complexity Levels:
- 99202/99212: Straightforward - minimal presenting problem, minimal decision-making
- 99203/99213: Low complexity - low-risk treatment options, limited data review
- 99204/99214: Moderate complexity - multiple chronic conditions, prescription drug management
- 99205/99215: High complexity - severe exacerbations, drug therapy requiring intensive monitoring

RED FLAGS for E/M Upcoding:
- Provider billing 99215 (highest E/M) for >20% of all visits (national average: ~5%)
- Identical E/M level for every patient regardless of complexity
- E/M code billed same day as procedure without documented separate reason

2. HCPCS PROCEDURE CODE BILLING RULES
- 99221 (Initial Hospital Care, low complexity): Avg Medicare payment ~$55-$75
- 99222 (Initial Hospital Care, moderate complexity): Avg Medicare payment ~$90-$110
- 99223 (Initial Hospital Care, high complexity): Avg Medicare payment ~$140-$160
- 93000 (ECG with interpretation): Avg Medicare payment ~$15-$20
- 71046 (Chest X-Ray 2 views): Avg Medicare payment ~$25-$35

3. FRAUD AND ABUSE INDICATORS
HIGH RISK patterns:
- Billing for services not rendered (phantom billing)
- Unbundling: billing separately for services that should be billed together
- Place of service errors: billing facility rates for services in office setting
- Excessive units: billing more units of a service than clinically reasonable
- Duplicate billing: submitting same claim more than once

STATISTICAL THRESHOLDS:
- Provider billing more than 3 standard deviations above peer average for any code = audit trigger
- Average submitted charge exceeding national benchmark by >400% = high fraud risk
- Total services per beneficiary per year >50 for same procedure code = high fraud risk

4. SPECIALTY-SPECIFIC BENCHMARKS
Hospitalists (Average per patient per admission):
- Expected E/M services: 3-7 visits per admission
- Expected charges: $200-$500 per admission
- Red flag: >$1,500 per patient per single admission

Radiologists:
- Most procedures: 1 read per study
- Red flag: Multiple reads of same study billed

Cardiologists:
- Stress tests (93015): typically once per year per patient
- Red flag: Monthly stress tests for same patient

5. PARTICIPATING PROVIDER RULES
- Participating providers must accept Medicare assignment
- Cannot charge more than Medicare-approved amount
- Non-participating providers can charge up to 115% of approved amount
- Limiting charge violations are subject to civil monetary penalties

6. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- All services must be documented within 24 hours of service
- Signatures: must be legible, dated, and timed
- Teaching physician rules: must personally document involvement
- Electronic health records: audit trail must be maintained
