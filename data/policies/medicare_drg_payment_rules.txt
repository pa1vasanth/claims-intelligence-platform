MEDICARE INPATIENT PROSPECTIVE PAYMENT SYSTEM (IPPS) - DRG PAYMENT RULES

1. OVERVIEW
Medicare pays hospitals for inpatient services using a fixed rate per Diagnosis Related Group (DRG).
The DRG payment is based on the patient's principal diagnosis, secondary diagnoses, procedures,
age, sex, and discharge status.

2. HIGH-COST DRG CATEGORIES AND EXPECTED CHARGE RATIOS
- DRG 001-013 (Nervous System, Complex): Expected charge-to-payment ratio: 4x to 8x
- DRG 003 (ECMO/Tracheostomy with MV >96 hours): Avg payment ~$80,000-$120,000
- DRG 023 (Craniotomy with Major Device): Avg payment ~$35,000-$50,000
- DRG 207 (Respiratory System Diagnoses): Avg payment ~$8,000-$15,000
- DRG 470 (Hip/Knee Replacement): Avg payment ~$12,000-$18,000
- DRG 871/872 (Septicemia): Avg payment ~$10,000-$25,000

3. FRAUD INDICATORS - INPATIENT CLAIMS
RED FLAGS requiring review:
- Charge-to-payment ratio exceeding 15:1 (industry norm is 3:1 to 8:1)
- Total discharges for rare DRGs exceeding 50 per year at a single facility
- Average submitted charge deviating more than 300% from state/national average
- Upcoding: billing higher-severity DRGs without documented clinical justification
- DRG creep: systematic pattern of assigning higher-weighted DRGs over time

4. OUTLIER PAYMENT POLICY
Outlier payments are made when the cost of treating a patient significantly exceeds the DRG payment.
Threshold: If the cost exceeds the DRG payment by more than the fixed-loss amount (~$38,788 in FY2024),
Medicare pays 80% of the excess. Abuse: Providers inflating charges to trigger outlier payments.

5. COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
- Medical records must support the DRG billed within 30 days of request
- All diagnoses must be present on admission (POA indicator required)
- Principal diagnosis: the condition chiefly responsible for admission after study
- CC/MCC (Complications/Comorbidities): must be documented and clinically validated

6. BUNDLING RULES
- Transfer cases: payment is reduced when patient transferred within 24 hours
- Same-day readmissions within 30 days: subject to review for inappropriate discharge
- Procedures performed at same facility on same day must be bundled
